Hackathon Rules:
Teams should build apps from scratch (Mobile App / Consumer Web App / Business Web App). Teams can solve as many “
Challenges
” as desired (zero, one, or multiple). Challenges are listed here:
https://api-cloud-ai-hackathon-2026.devpost.com/
Teams must be between 1 - 5 members. Teams with over 5 members are not eligible to win anything.
Participants are encouraged to form teams prior to the start of the Hackathon. Participants, from their DevPost accounts, may “
Look for Teammates
”.
Hackathon Workshops & Kickoff Talks will take place Monday, August 17  from 10:00 AM - 10:30 AM PST.
All “
Challenges
” are hosted by sponsors. The sponsors will choose their own prize and choose their own prize winners at the conclusion of the hackathon. Read this sheet to understand how sponsors judge.
To be considered for the Overall Winner - teams must submit their projects by Thursday, September 3 at 10:00 AM PST. No late projects will be accepted.
There will be two rounds of judging on Thursday During the Overall Judging- Round 1 (10:00 AM - 12:00 PM (PT) ), Overall Round Judges will review your projects and fill out a score card. The Sponsor Round - Round 2 (10:00 AM - 12:00 PM (PT)) Judges will review your projects and fill out a score card.
Overall Judges (Round 1) will evaluate you based on:
Progress
How much progress did you make?
Concept
Does it solve a real problem?
Feasibility
Could this become a startup or company?
The Top 5 project teams (as decided by the Overall Judges - Round 1) will pitch on the Main Stage to all attendees. You will be notified via phone call at 1:00 PM if you are selected in the Top 5 and must be onsite at 2:00pm, for the 2:30PM Top 5 Demo & Q&A
Sponsors are responsible for delivering their prizes to winners (
usually immediately after
). If needed, we will provide sponsors team contact information. Sponsor & Overall winners are announced at 3:30 PM on Thursday
